About Me

I’m a neuroscientist and data scientist working at the intersection of cognitive neuroscience, brain stimulation, and computational modeling. My research integrates neuroimaging, multivariate decoding (MVPA), and experimental design to investigate how the brain represents information, adapts to change, and supports behavior.

I focus on translating complex neural signals into interpretable models and reproducible pipelines that connect brain dynamics with cognition.
