About Me

II’m a researcher working at the intersection of neuroscience, brain stimulation, and data science. My work combines neuroimaging, multivariate decoding (MVPA), computational methods, and experimental approaches to study how the brain represents information, adapts to changing environments, and supports cognition.

I’m interested in turning complex neural data into interpretable models and building quantitative approaches that connect brain dynamics with behavior.
